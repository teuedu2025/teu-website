Welcome to TEU University

Situated in the vibrant coastal city of Vladivostok, TEU University combines modern academic excellence, state-of-the-art campus infrastructure, and a welcoming international environment.

Campus Grounds

Modern Vladivostok Campus

Located on Gogolya Street, our campus offers advanced lecture halls, high-tech research labs, and expansive green zones right in the heart of the city.
